Our Story

Born from Fire,
Raised on Flavor

Tucked into a restored 1920s firehouse, Saffron & Smoke began with a single oak-burning hearth and a simple belief: the best flavor is coaxed out slowly, never rushed. Chef Amara Reyes builds every dish around live fire — from ember-roasted vegetables to dry-aged steaks finished over smoldering hardwood.

We work with a small circle of local farms and fisheries, and the menu turns with the seasons. Saffron threads are bloomed by hand each morning, breads are proofed overnight, and every sauce begins with bones or vegetables roasted in the dying embers of the night before. It's slower cooking for a faster world.

— Chef Amara Reyes, Founder
