Atelier Notes

Made Slow, Worn Long

Veloura began in 2021 as a two-person studio above a tailor’s shop in Antwerp, with one pattern, one bolt of silk and a stubborn belief: clothes should outlive trends. Today we work with 26 family-run ateliers across Europe, cutting every style in batches of forty or fewer.

We choose fabrics the way you would choose friends — natural, honest, better with age. Mulberry silk, double-faced wool, vegetable-tanned leather. Nothing synthetic pretending to be something it is not. What we make is meant to be repaired, re-lined and re-loved, never replaced.
