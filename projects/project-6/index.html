Features

Everything your ops team wishes it had

Six primitives that turn disconnected apps into one smooth, observable system — with humans in the loop exactly where they belong.

Workflow Automation

Drag triggers, actions and conditions into place. Flowbit runs them on a schedule or in realtime, retrying gracefully whenever a step fails.

Realtime Analytics

Live dashboards track every run, hand-off and bottleneck. Know the second a flow slows down instead of hearing about it in standup.

Team Inbox

Approvals, alerts and exceptions land in one shared inbox. Assign, comment and resolve without ever leaving Flowbit.

Smart Reports

Weekly digests that write themselves. Send the metrics that matter to Slack, email or Notion — formatted and always on time.

Integrations

120+ native connectors from Stripe to HubSpot, plus webhooks and a full REST API for everything else in your stack.

Enterprise Security

SOC 2 Type II, SSO/SAML, granular roles and audit logs. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, always.
