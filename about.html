My story

From curiosity to craft

My journey started the way most good things do — with curiosity. I wanted to know how the websites I admired every day were actually made. So I opened the inspector, broke a few pages (including my own), and slowly taught myself to put them back together better than before.

Today, as a student completing my Matriculation while pursuing a 3-year software diploma at Aptech, I balance structured computer education with thousands of hours of hands-on building. Nexora Labz is where that learning becomes real products for real people.

I believe great websites are more than code — they're rhythm, motion and clarity. That belief shapes every project that leaves this studio.
